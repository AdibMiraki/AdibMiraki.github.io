01   /   ABOUT ME

Giving cells a
digital voice.

I study how cells behave, interact, and evolve across different biological contexts.

I'm a Bioinformatician at the Lotfollahi Lab, Wellcome Sanger Institute. I develop and apply AI-driven frameworks to uncover cellular heterogeneity and perturbation responses in complex tissues.

My research brings together machine learning, single-cell genomics, and spatial transcriptomics to decode the language of cells — and let data science do the talking.
